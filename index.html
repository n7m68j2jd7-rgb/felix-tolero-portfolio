Your content shouldn’t
be the bottleneck.

Good creative work feels obvious, because the hard work behind it stays hidden. Felix Mari Tolero

Most clients arrive with the same bottleneck. The ideas are there and the recording is done — then it stalls. Episodes sit unedited for weeks. Campaign posts go out looking like they came from four different brands. The pitch deck gets built the night before the pitch.

I’m Felix Mari Tolero, a graphic designer and video editor based in Cebu City, Philippines, working with clients remotely. I take that production load off you end to end: podcast editing and publishing, social media campaigns, short-form reels, presentation design, and Webflow and WordPress builds.

The fix is rarely talent — it’s system. Every project starts from a template and a fixed file structure: one type hierarchy, one colour system, one naming convention. So the tenth asset costs a fraction of the first and still looks like it belongs beside it. That is why a six-post campaign reads as one campaign, and why a 25-slide deck holds from cover to close.

I hold a BS in Computer Science from the University of Cebu, which is where the systems habit comes from. Since 2022 I have delivered work for podcasters, authors, property developers and non-profits — seven shows edited, and the eight projects above.

Pixel art is the same habit in another form. Aseprite sits in my toolbox next to Photoshop, and this site follows the same rule: everything on an integer grid, nothing blurry.
